// about

Numbers first, story second.

I’m a finance student who enjoys equity research, valuation and financial modelling. My work usually starts with a company’s annual report or a raw market dataset and ends with a clear, defendable view.

I did my B.Com at Amity University (2025) and I’m now doing my PGDM in Financial Management at FIIB Delhi (2026–28). Alongside the course I’m preparing for CFA Level 1.

Outside finance I build small web tools, like a canteen ordering app and a voting system, because I like making things people actually use.
